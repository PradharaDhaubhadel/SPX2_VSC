SINCE 2004

Built for the whole city.

City Mall opened with 14 shops and a single food stall. Two decades later, it's the meeting point for families, teenagers, and everyone shopping for the specific thing they need.

City Mall was built on one idea: a mall should feel like the city itself — a little loud, a little layered, and big enough that everyone finds their corner of it. Jewelers sit two doors from an arcade. A cinema shares a floor with a bookshop. That mix is deliberate.

We're independently run, and every shop inside is either a founding tenant or a local business we sought out. We don't lease to chains that already have five locations across town — we'd rather give the space to the ice cream shop that only exists here.

Today the mall spans three floors and a ground-level grocery wing, with sixty-two shops across twelve categories, and a calendar that stays full — carnivals, tournaments, tastings, and fashion weeks run through most months of the year.
